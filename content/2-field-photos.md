═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 ★ HOW TO ADD A DESCRIPTION TO A PHOTO  ★
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Right now none of your photos have descriptions — they are shown on their own.

Every photo below has two OPTIONAL lines waiting for you:

    Label:
    Description:

Fill either one in and a caption panel appears under that photo, with a
coloured line across the top. Leave both blank and the photo stays plain.

    Label:        a few words — a place, a date, a kind of work.
                  Shown small, in capitals, in blue above the description.
    Description:  a sentence or two, in the serif type.

You can fill these in for some photos and not others. Mixing is fine — photos
without a description simply have no panel.

EXAMPLE — this is what a filled-in photo looks like:

    ## Photo: mah14.jpg
    Label: Nandurbar, Maharashtra
    Description: Two women I sat with for most of an afternoon. Neither of them
    called what they did all day "work".

Your photos are never cropped. Whatever shape a photo is, it is shown whole,
so a description never covers any part of the image.

TO ADD A NEW PHOTO
  1. Put the photo file into the "field" folder.
  2. Copy any three-line block below and paste it at the bottom.
  3. Change the file name, and fill in the Label and Description if you want them.

Type the file name exactly as it appears in the field folder. All your photo
files now end in lowercase .jpg, so keep new ones lowercase too — the web
server treats Mah8.JPG and mah8.jpg as two different files even though your
Mac does not.
